Every Perfect Week

All five teams have to play, and all five have to win, in the same week of the college football season. A bye doesn't break the streak — it just means that team wasn't in play that week, so the week doesn't count toward the total either way. Click any week to see the final scores.

Who Ruins It Most

Counted only in weeks where all five teams actually played and exactly one of them lost — the clean, undisputed spoiler.

Teams tracked: Maryland, Wisconsin, Missouri, Virginia Tech, Georgia. Week numbers follow the college football season calendar (Week 1 starts with each team's season opener), not the plain calendar year.
